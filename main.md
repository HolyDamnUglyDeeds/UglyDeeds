# 認真的
<font size="5"><b>Seriously.</b></font>


我說過我很膽小，我很怕，我怕到快要瘋掉了。  
I told you, I’m a coward. I’m scared. And I'm so scared that I feel like I’m about to lose my mind.  
  
如果我沒有繼續更新，就是因為不屈服而被處理掉了。  
If I stop posting updates, it means they dealt with me for refusing to give in like always.

為何前幾次都沒事，這次會這麼特別?  
Previous instances similarly involved all ugly deeds, yet remained non-fatal. Why is this specific occurrence perceived as fatal?


## 恩惠
<font size="4"><b>favor</b></font>  

<br>  


因為有警員當面告訴我，他的職務就是**施加恩惠給民眾**。  
Because a police officer told me to my face that his job was to **grant favors to the public**.

==我懇請你們去調勤務密錄器的影片，看完上下文，我懇請你們告訴我，我的理解有誤。==  
==I implore you to review the officers’ body-camera footage. Watch the full context, then tell me I’m wrong.==

同時。  
Meanwhile, 

警員還喝斥手機竊案的**被害人陳述**對電視牆的**意見**，連續喝問：**你在找我麻煩是不是? 你在找我麻煩是不是?**  
The officer also berated the **victim** of the phone theft for **expressing an opinion** about the video wall, repeatedly yelling, **“Are you trying to give me trouble? Are you trying to give me trouble?”**

同時。  
And at the same time,

還在被害人製作報案筆錄時，**阻止被害人自由陳述**，**筆錄只能記錄下警員同意的文字。**  
As the victim，me,  **gave my statement**, i **was stopped from stating freely**; **the report** ended up **recording only what the officer allowed**. **For Each And Every Question**.

他的長官曾經阻止他。但在幾個問題之後，他又依然故我，**阻止被害人正面回覆他的問題**。  
His superior had once tried to check him. Yet, after a few more questions, he remained obstinate, **silencing the victim's direct replies**.

==我懇求你們去調閱筆錄錄影，看完之後告訴我，我的理解有錯。==  
==I implore you to review the recordings of the statement; then tell me that my understanding of what happened is wrong.==

當我兩天後去問案情進度，他否認他問過的問題，質疑被害人謊報竊案，說他怎麼知道我的手機是不是真的被偷?  
Two days later, when I followed up on the case, he disavowed his own questions and cast doubt on the theft, wondering aloud how he could possibly know if the phone was truly stolen?

<br><br>  

請問，你對台灣公務人員的印象如何?  
What is your impression of Taiwanese public servants?
  
敢動不動就喝斥你的，你見過幾個?  
How many have you encountered who frequently resort to intense verbal reprimands?
  
敢動不動就用嚇你來欺壓你的，你見過幾個?  
How many have you encountered who employ intimidation as a means of oppression?

來板橋，去派出所，多說幾句。  
Visit a police station in Banqiao and engage in conversation. 

你就會見到全台灣最囂張的公務員。  
You will encounter the most arrogant civil servants in Taiwan.

將近十年前。  
Approximately ten years ago,   

我停在路邊的機車被車禍波及， 
my motorcycle, which was parked on the roadside, was impacted by a traffic accident. 

警員留下字條，說明車損原因，以及員警的聯絡方式。  
A police officer left a note specifying the cause of the damage and providing their contact details.  

我到警察局後，該名員警不在，  
Upon my arrival at the police station, the officer mentioned in the note was not present. 

我說明來意，想確認我是否被捲入那場車禍中，
I stated my objective: to verify whether I had been implicated in the aforementioned accident.  

值班員警說的很含糊，聽起來像是不會，  
The officer on duty provided an ambiguous response, suggesting that I had not been. 

我想確認，他當場立即改用喝斥的口氣。  
When I sought further confirmation, the officer immediately transitioned to a reprimanding tone.  

<br><br>  

如果你想見識全台灣最囂張的公務員，來板橋，去派出所，多說幾句。  
To witness the highest degree of arrogance among Taiwanese civil servants, one may visit a police station in Banqiao and engage in a prolonged conversation.
